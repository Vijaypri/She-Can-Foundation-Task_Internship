✍️ a little about us

We started small.
We started with heart.

In 2015, a small group of women sat in a living room and asked a simple question: why do so many brilliant women disappear before they even get started? The answers — poverty, isolation, lack of mentors, systems that never saw them coming — became our to-do list.

Today we work in 38 countries, but we've never stopped thinking in terms of one person at a time. Because every statistic we share is a woman with a name, a family, a dream she's been nurturing quietly.

We're not just an NGO. We're a community of women who've been there — and women who are going there — learning from each other, cheering each other on, and refusing to leave anyone behind.

"The mentorship programme didn't give me a job. It gave me the confidence to go get one myself. That's the difference."

— Amara O., programme graduate, Lagos
